# Lighting (quick reference)

Values are practical home targets, rounded. Workplace tables (such as EN 12464-1) are stricter; use them as a ceiling, not a floor, for homes.

## Lux targets

| Space / task | General (ambient) | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Living room | 100–200 | reading 300–500 |
| Bedroom | 100–150 | bedside reading 300 |
| Kids' room | 150–300 | homework 500 |
| Study / desk | 200–300 | 500 |
| Kitchen | 200–300 | worktop 300–500 |
| Dining table | 150–200 | over table 200–300 |
| Bathroom | 150–200 | mirror 300–500 |
| Hall / corridor | 100 | – |
| Stairs | 100–150 | – |
| Balcony | 50–100 | – |

## Quick lumen formula

`lumens needed ≈ area (m²) × target lux × 1.25`

The 1.25 covers losses from shades, dark walls and distance. Use 1.5 for very dark walls or ceilings over 3 m.

Example: bedroom 12 m² at 150 lux → 12 × 150 × 1.25 = 2250 lm → for example, one 1500 lm ceiling light plus two 400 lm bedside lamps.

Rough bulb guide (LED): 470 lm ≈ old 40 W, 800 lm ≈ old 60 W, 1500 lm ≈ old 100 W.

## Colour temperature (kelvin)

| K | Feeling | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 2200–2700 | very warm, candle-like | accent, evening, bedroom |
| 2700–3000 | warm white | living, bedroom, dining |
| 3000–3500 | neutral warm | kitchen, bathroom |
| 4000 | neutral / cool white | desk, workshop, wardrobe |
| 5000+ | daylight, cold | avoid at home except garage or crafts |

Keep one colour temperature per room for ambient light; mix only for task or accent.

## Three layers

1. **Ambient:** ceiling light, flush mount, or a few spots. Dimmable if possible.
2. **Task:** desk lamp (light from the side opposite the writing hand), under-cabinet LED strip in the kitchen, mirror light at face height on both sides, reading lamp behind the shoulder.
3. **Accent:** LED strip in shelves, picture light, a floor lamp in a dark corner, plants lit from below.

## Good habits

- CRI 90+ bulbs show skin, food and paint colours truly.
- Avoid one bright light in the middle of the ceiling as the only source.
- Plug-in lamps and battery or USB LED strips need no electrician. Anything wired into the wall does.
- Window side: north light is even and cool; south is strong and warm; east is morning sun; west is hot afternoon sun. Plan curtains or blinds for south and west.
