# Example: rented study-bedroom, 2.8 × 3.2 m

Fictional user. Shows a full answer, shortened for a phone.

## Request

> I rent a room in Baku, 2.8 × 3.2 m, ceiling 2.7 m. Window 1.4 m wide in the middle of a short wall, faces north. Door 80 cm in the corner of the opposite wall. I need a single bed, a desk and a wardrobe. The landlord says I can paint. Budget 600 AZN. I like calm and warm.

## Result

**Renter mode:** no drilling beyond what the landlord allows; keep his written OK and take move-in photos.

**1. Summary** — 9 m², north light. Bed along the left wall, desk under the window, wardrobe by the door wall. Warm light colours.

**2. Plan** — scale: 1 char = 20 cm across, 1 line = 40 cm down
```
+------=======-+
|BBBBB  KKKKKK |
|BBBBB  KKKKKK |
|BBBBB ..  CC  |
|BBBBB ..      |
|BBBBB ..      |
|###   ..      |
|###    ..     |
|###      .... |
+---------DDDD-+
```
`B` bed 100 × 200 · `K` desk 120 × 60 (drawn 2 lines, rounded) · `C` chair · `#` wardrobe 120 × 60, doors open to the right · `===` window · `DDDD` door 80 cm · `.` walkway.
Checks: bed side to chair 80 cm (min 75 ✅); in front of wardrobe 220 cm ✅; bed foot touches the wardrobe side — fine for a single bed you enter from the side.

**3. Palette (60-30-10), warm for north light**
- A: walls warm white `#F3EDE2`, wood/oat `#C8B79E`, terracotta `#B5654A`
- B: walls soft sand `#E8DCC8`, olive `#8A8B6A`, mustard `#D2A24C`
- C: walls pale peach `#F2E1D3`, warm grey `#9E958C`, deep teal `#2F5D62`
Test a sample on two walls in day and evening light.

**4. Light** — 9 m² × 150 lux × 1.25 ≈ 1,700 lm → one 1,500 lm ceiling light (2700–3000 K) + a 400–500 lm desk lamp (4000 K) on the left if you write with your right hand. Plug-in only — no electrician needed.

**5. Paint** — walls 12.0 m × 2.7 = 32.4 m² − door 1.8 − window 2.0 = 28.6 m² × 2 coats ÷ 10 m²/L ≈ 5.7 L → **buy 6 L** (check coverage on the tin).

**6. Shopping (approx. AZN)**
- Basic: paint + roller set 70–100 · desk lamp 25–40 · curtain on a tension rod 40–60 · second-hand desk (tap.az) 60–100
- Better: new mattress 200–300 (buy new) · rug 60–90
- Later: shelf on the wardrobe top, plants
- Contingency 10%: 60
